About Erik

BORN TO RACE.

Frederik Rahn Stampe was born on March 3, 2008 in Gothenburg, Sweden. He got his first dirt bike at age 5, and by 9 he was lining up at his first competitive races. What started as a childhood passion quickly revealed itself as something much more serious.

At 17, Erik is already a three-time national champion and the current EMX250 championship leader with 8 race wins in 2025 alone. Riding for Stampe Racing on a KTM 250 SX-F, he combines fearless aggression with technical precision that coaches describe as rare for his age.

Off the bike, Erik is focused, disciplined, and deeply aware of the importance of building a brand that lasts beyond the race track. He trains year-round in Sweden and Belgium, and has become a role model for the next generation of Scandinavian motocross talent.

Career

THE JOURNEY

From Peewee class in the Swedish countryside to championship leader on the European stage.

2018
First Race — Peewee Class
Started racing competitively at age 9 in the Swedish Peewee class. Won his very first regional event.
2019
Regional Champion — Mini Class
Dominated the Mini class in the Central Sweden region, winning 7 of 9 rounds and clinching his first championship title.
2020
Swedish National Junior Champion
Stepped up to National Junior level and claimed the Swedish championship, announcing himself as the country's brightest young talent.
2021
First International Podium — EMX125
Made his international debut in the EMX125 class and scored a P3 at Valkenswaard — his very first European podium at age 13.
2022
EMX125 — Top 5 Overall
Consistent season in EMX125, finishing P4 in the overall standings with 3 podiums and establishing himself as a future star.
2023
Graduated to EMX250 — Immediate Podium
Moved up to the 250cc class ahead of schedule. Scored a podium in just his third outing, shocking the paddock with his pace.
2024
3 Wins · P3 Championship
Breakout season with 3 race victories and a P3 championship finish. Signed with Stampe Racing and Fox Racing for the 2025 campaign.
2025
8 Wins · #1 Championship Leader
Historic season so far — 8 wins from 8 rounds, championship leader heading into the season finale. A champion in waiting.
